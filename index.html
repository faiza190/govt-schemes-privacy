Privacy Policy — Govt Schemes Pakistan

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version: This app does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. No account, no login, no ads, no analytics.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personal information, and it does not track how you use the app.

2. Schemes you add yourself

If you add a scheme using the in-app Add tab, that information is stored only on your own device. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared with us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

3. Internet use

The app uses the internet only when you tap a link to open an official government scheme website in your browser. We do not see or record which links you open.

4. Saved items & settings

Your saved (bookmarked) schemes and app settings (such as language choice) are stored only on your device.

5. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
